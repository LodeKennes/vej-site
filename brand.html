Voice

State in, probabilities out.

Precise, composed, candid. Name what the model returns. Say what a result measures. Keep probability values distinct from expected rubric positions.

“The decision layer you own” is the supporting line. Avoid unmeasured speed, calibrated-certainty or superiority claims. Vegetable language belongs in the mark; the documentation stays concrete.

Website privacy

This notice covers access requests, waitlists, newsletter signups and contact messages.

What we save

Vej saves your email and the request or list you choose. Name and company are optional. Newsletter entries include your consent and signup date. A contact message includes your email, optional name, the message and your consent.

How we use it

We use access requests to arrange Pea access. Waitlist entries receive updates about the selected model. Newsletter entries receive Vej updates. Contact messages are used to reply.

Storage

Cloudflare processes the forms and browser checks. The signup database stores records in the EU. These website records are separate from model inputs and the hosted API’s content policy.

Changes and removal

We keep access details while we handle your request, list entries while you remain on the list, and contact messages while we handle the reply. Use the contact form or email hello@itsvej.com to change or remove your details. Deleted records can remain in Cloudflare’s database recovery backup for up to 30 days.

The hosted API has a data processing agreement.

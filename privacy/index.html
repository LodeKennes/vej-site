Website privacy

This notice covers access requests, waitlists and newsletter signups.

What we save

Vej saves your email and the request or list you choose. Name and company are optional. Newsletter entries include your consent and signup date.

How we use it

We use access requests to arrange Pea access. Waitlist entries receive updates about the selected model. Newsletter entries receive Vej updates.

Storage

Cloudflare processes the forms and browser checks. The signup database stores records in the EU. These website records are separate from model inputs and the hosted API’s content policy.

Changes and removal

We keep access details while we handle your request, and list entries while you remain on the list. Contact hello@itsvej.com to change or remove your details. Deleted records can remain in Cloudflare’s database recovery backup for up to 30 days.

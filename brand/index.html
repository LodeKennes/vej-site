Model marks

Three names. Their own shapes.

The model marks share flat colour, open cuts and botanical silhouettes. Pea’s compact horizontal pod shows three round peas and a curling tendril; Vej keeps its vertical open-pod company logo. Pea is the current model; Pepper (8B) and Pumpkin (27B) are in research and development, with private company testing.

Download the original SVGs above. The shapes remain recognisable at small sizes, and the stems adapt to the visitor’s colour scheme. Model colours belong to the artwork; status labels use the normal text palette.

Voice

State in, probabilities out.

Precise, composed, candid. Name what the model returns. Say what a result measures. Keep probability values distinct from expected rubric positions.

“The decision layer you own” is the supporting line. Avoid unmeasured speed, calibrated-certainty or superiority claims. One dry aside per page is welcome. Vegetable jokes are allowed in small doses, never in a sentence that carries a number.

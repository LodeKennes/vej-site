What a sprout does

Helpers that keep going while you get on with your day

Sprouts runs as an app on your Mac. Each sprout keeps its own memory, rhythms and private computer between conversations, and works with the AI you already pay for, like ChatGPT or Claude.

  • Recurring work, done

    Ask once, in plain words. Rhythms run on schedule: a morning digest, a weekly check, a reminder you would otherwise forget.

    See a sprout at work →

  • Its own computer

    Every sprout gets a private computer of its own, with a web browser and its own files. Sprouts sets it up for you.

    Look inside →

  • You stay in charge

    A sprout asks before it acts in your apps. Passwords stay locked on your Mac and never appear in the chat.

    How it stays safe →

  • On your iPhone

    Notifications when a sprout has news or needs you, and every conversation in your pocket.

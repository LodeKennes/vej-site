Introducing the Vej Journal

A place for release notes, research updates and the reasoning behind what we build.

Lode Kennes15 October 2026 · 1 min read

On this page

What you will find hereFollowing along

Vej moves quickly. New capabilities reach the hosted API, evaluations get published and models move through development. Until now, most of those changes appeared quietly on the pages they touched. The Journal gives each one a dated write-up with a name attached.

What you will find here

  • Release notes for new features on the hosted API, such as endpoints, field types and limits.
  • Research updates on Pea, Pepper and Pumpkin, with links to the published evidence where it exists.
  • Development notes on decisions we made, including the ones we decided not to ship.

We hold every post to the same standard as the rest of the site. Measured results link to their evaluation. Development results are labelled as development results. Work that is still private stays private until there is something we can stand behind.

Following along

Subscribe to the RSS feed in any feed reader, or join the newsletter for occasional updates by email. Questions about a post are welcome through the contact form.
